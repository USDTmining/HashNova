Frequently Asked Questions
Is this a real mining platform?

No. HashNova is a UI/UX prototype. All balances, contracts, hashrates and transactions are simulated in the browser. No real cryptocurrency is mined or transferred.

How do cloud mining contracts work (in production)?

In a real product, users purchase a fixed amount of hashrate for a defined period. The provider operates physical miners and distributes proportional rewards. This demo only mimics the interface and flow.

Can I withdraw real money?

No. Withdraw and deposit buttons update local demo state only. No blockchain or payment rails are connected.

What algorithms are supported?

This prototype focuses on SHA-256 (Bitcoin-style) cloud contracts. A production version could add more algorithms.
