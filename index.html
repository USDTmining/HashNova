Frequently Asked Questions
Is this a real trading platform?

No. HashNova is presented here as a browser-only demo platform. Market execution and client-money movement remain disabled until authorized MT5/LP/payment integrations are connected.

How does HashNova connect to a broker?

In a real product, users purchase a fixed amount of hashrate for a defined period. The provider operates physical miners and distributes proportional rewards. This demo only mimics the interface and flow.

Can I withdraw real money?

No. Withdraw and deposit buttons update server-backed account state only. No blockchain or payment rails are connected.

What markets are supported?

The catalog is structured for Forex, indices, stocks, ETFs, bonds, energies, metals, commodities and crypto where the connected broker supplies the instrument.
